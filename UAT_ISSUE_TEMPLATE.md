## Severity definitions

| Severity | Definition |
|---|---|
| **BLOCKER** | Data loss, unauthorized access, a wrong published academic result, an incorrect payment balance, or a core workflow that cannot be completed at all. |
| **HIGH** | A serious integrity, security, or workflow risk with realistic impact — not yet causing visibly wrong output, but a real problem under plausible conditions. |
| **MEDIUM** | A genuine workflow problem, but one with a usable workaround. |
| **LOW** | Minor usability, wording, or layout issue. |
| **ENHANCEMENT** | Nothing is incorrect — this is a suggestion for something better. |

When unsure between two severities, pick the higher one and let the UAT coordinator downgrade it — it's easier to relax a BLOCKER than to notice a missed one.
